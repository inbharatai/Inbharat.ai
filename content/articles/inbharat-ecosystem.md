# The InBharat.ai Ecosystem: A Historical Twelve-Product Portfolio

Building for Bharat means considering language, connectivity, cost and domain needs together. That motivates a portfolio rather than a claim that every product shares one account, one database or one model.

## The twelve products in the June 2026 article

- **InBharat AI:** the separate browser search and chat console, not today’s PAI flagship.
- **KathaKitaab:** interactive stories and AI-assisted storytelling.
- **JAK Swarm:** open-source, self-hostable agent workflows with evidence graphs, review gates and audit trails.
- **Sahayaak AI:** a personal AI workspace project spanning chat, voice and document workflows.
- **Sahayaak Seva:** field-assistance work for Anganwadi-related workflows; not a claim of government endorsement or clinically validated care.
- **UniAssist.ai:** international-admissions guidance, university matching and scholarship discovery; not guaranteed admissions or immigration outcomes.
- **TestsPrep.in:** exam preparation, practice and performance analytics.
- **UniBot:** conversational support oriented toward WhatsApp and student guidance.
- **Phoring:** document-based decision intelligence and simulations, not guaranteed forecasts.
- **Agent Arcade:** agent observability, session replay and cost-analysis tooling.
- **SocialFlow:** social generation and publishing automation. Local encrypted credentials do not keep published content or cloud-generation requests on-device.
- **OpenClawFix:** developer setup tooling. Installation time and supported providers depend on the version and environment.

The [current product directory](/#products) retains these projects and also lists UnoOne, JAK Shield and SwasthyaScore AI. Their availability, clinical applicability, supported languages and integrations must be checked individually, not inferred from a portfolio listing.

## Today’s flagship: PAI through UnoOne

[PAI](/private-ai), pronounced “Pi”, is one personal private AI agent. [UnoOne](/unoone) implements it through Power for Windows and Mobile for Android. Power is a development build; Mobile is an alpha. No public installer is offered, and a physical phone–desktop sync round trip is not yet qualified. The older removable-drive Pocket AI formulation is historical research, not the current consumer product.

## Shared principles are not shared personal data

The earlier article described an Aadhaar-based identity layer and connections between health records, learning data, search history and commerce. No verified implementation, consent specification or authorized Aadhaar relationship was provided with this review. Those descriptions must not be relied on as existing features or approved integrations.

Likewise, a company built in India does not establish that all processing or hosting stays in India. Location depends on each product, provider, configuration and contract. A regional-processing promise needs an audited data-flow map and applicable provider terms.

A future integration would need purpose-specific consent, access controls, deletion rules and explicit data boundaries. Similar engineering approaches across products do not imply that models train on a combined pool of user data.

## Availability and responsible use

Public sites and repositories provide entry points, but do not prove production deployments. The earlier “in the field with Anganwadi workers” and “all usable now” statements require owner-supplied dated evidence. Ask the team about the specific product and engagement rather than treating this historical article as an availability guarantee.

Health-related tools require clinical validation and professional oversight for their intended use. They are not substitutes for medical diagnosis, measurement or emergency care. Programme participation and company recognition do not certify products.

## Why a portfolio can still make sense

A learner may need stories, exam practice and admissions guidance at different stages. An organisation may need both agent workflows and observability. A portfolio can address those related needs without silently linking accounts or personal records. The strategy is to build useful tools with clear boundaries, then validate integrations deliberately.
